<h1 align="center">FRY OR DIE</h1>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="60%" valign="top" align="center">

In <b>FRY OR DIE</b>, you and a rival chef toss ingredients on a shared table. Whoever fills their stars first wins. You're Bob, a tiny cook up against pros. Simple, chaotic, and hilarious!

<img src="https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzI4ODU1NTEyLmdpZg==/original/XdaBxI.gif" width="100%">

<img src="https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzI4ODU4OTM4LnBuZw==/original/rDW6SX.png" width="100%">


Every round is timed - so hurry! Drag tasty stuff to your side, push the nasty rotten food to the other chef, and don't let go of your treasures because hooks, tongs, and even cleavers will try to yank them away. Pure kitchen madness!

<img src="https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzI4ODU5MDU2LmdpZg==/original/gdNR%2FW.gif" width="100%">

Hit the supermarket to grab fresh ingredients for your fridge, and pick up spices and sauces that offer passive perks!
